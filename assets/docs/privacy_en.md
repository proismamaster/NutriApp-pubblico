# Privacy Policy
**under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) - NutriApp**

## 1. Data Controller
The Data Controller is **NutriApp Team**, based in Italy.
For any information regarding the processing of personal data, you can contact the Controller at: privacy@nutriapp.it.

## 2. Types of Data Processed
The following categories of personal data may be processed:
**Identification and Contact Data**: name, email address, login credentials.
**Health Data (special categories under Art. 9 GDPR)**: weight, height, nutritional goals, dietary habits, caloric intake data, and any info voluntarily provided.
**Usage Data**: app interaction and preferences.
**Technical Data**: IP address, device IDs, OS, log data.

## 3. Purposes of Processing
Personal data are processed to:
- Provide nutritional monitoring and food diary services;
- Allow account creation and management;
- Generate personalized statistics and charts;
- Improve app quality and features;
- Fulfill legal obligations;
- Provide technical support.

## 4. Legal Basis
Processing is based on:
- Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: contract execution;
- Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR: legal obligations;
- Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR: user consent;
- Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR: explicit consent for health data.

## 5. Processing Methods
Data is processed using IT tools following principles of lawfulness, fairness, transparency, and data minimization.

## 6. Retention Period
Personal data will be kept for the time strictly necessary to achieve the purposes for which they were collected. Health data will be kept exclusively for the time necessary to provide the requested service.

## 7. Data Communication
Personal data may be shared with IT service providers, cloud infrastructures, and legal/tax consultants.

## 7a. Community Contributions
If you propose a food or a recipe to the public database, or send a report, the content is reviewed by a NutriApp administrator, who sees your name and email.

An **approved food or recipe becomes part of the NutriApp database**: it stays visible to other users even if you remove it from your library, and you can no longer withdraw or edit it. If it contains a mistake, you can ask for a correction by sending a report.

Other users only see your first name, never your email or surname. The likes you give to public recipes are linked to your account; other users only see the total.

You can also like your own public recipes; the author's own like does not count towards the recommendation score.

When you propose a correction you can suggest nutrition values, the name, the brand, the category, the serving, the ingredients and a product photo, attaching label photos as evidence. Photos are uploaded to our servers and are seen by whoever reviews the report. The administrator can accept **only some** of the proposed fields: accepted ones become part of the NutriApp database and stay there even if you delete your account.

If you report an app issue we keep the description, the screen the report came from, and the administrator's reply, which you read in "My reports".

## 7b. Cookies and similar technologies
The app uses no cookies and no advertising identifiers: preferences stay on your phone. The web admin panel uses a single technical session cookie, needed to keep you signed in: it expires with the session and is not used for profiling.

## 8. User Rights
Users can exercise rights under Arts. 15–22 GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) by contacting privacy@nutriapp.it.

## 9. Data Security
NutriApp adopts adequate technical and organizational measures under Art. 32 GDPR.

## 10. Minors
The application is not intended for children under 14 years of age.

## 11. Changes to this Policy
This privacy policy may be updated over time. Substantial changes will be communicated to users.
