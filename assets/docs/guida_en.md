# User Manual - NutriApp

Welcome to NutriApp! This quick guide will help you navigate the main features of the application.

## 1. Navigation Bar
The bottom bar allows you to easily move between sections:
- **Home**: Daily summary, remaining calories, and macronutrients.
- **Calendar**: Monthly view showing the days you logged meals.
- **Add (+)**: Quick entry of a new food item.
- **Recipes**: Your saved and custom recipes.
- **Charts**: Nutritional statistics over time (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly).

## 2. Food Entry
Press the **+** button from the Home screen or the navigation bar.
- **Search**: Find the food in the global database.
- **Barcode Scan**: Use the camera to scan a product's barcode.
- **Manual**: If you can't find a food, you can register it manually by entering its macronutrients.
- **Recipes**: Add a saved recipe with a single tap.

## 3. My Goals
In Settings (gear icon in the top right), you can access "My Goals".
Here you can define:
- Your current weight and target weight.
- Your daily calorie goal.
- The breakdown of macronutrients (Carbohydrates, Proteins, Fats).
- Specific limits for vitamins and minerals.

## 4. Statistics and Charts
Access the Charts section to monitor your progress.
- View your calorie trend over time.
- Analyze the percentage distribution of macronutrients.
- Export data as a PDF to share or print.

## 5. Account Settings
From the Settings menu you can:
- Edit your profile (name, age, gender, height, photo).
- Change units of measurement (kg/lb, cm/ft).
- Manage notifications to remind you to drink water or log meals.
- Change the application language.
